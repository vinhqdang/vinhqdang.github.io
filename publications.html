How to read the indexing badges

WoS is the Web of Science edition (SCIE, ESCI, SSCI). Scopus is the CiteScore percentile in the venue's best-ranked subject category where Scopus publishes one; most book series and proceedings are indexed without a percentile. SJR is the best SCImago Journal Rank quartile. A badge appears only where the status was confirmed against the primary database. A missing badge means not indexed, not applicable to that source type, or not confirmed.

Indexing status cross-checked against SCImago, the Web of Science Master Journal List and Scopus source data.
