Methodology

How the indexing columns work

WoS shows the Web of Science category (SCIE, ESCI, SSCI) where confirmed. Scopus shows whether the venue is an active Scopus source. Scimago shows the best SCImago Journal Rank quartile (Q1–Q4). Where a venue's status could not be independently confirmed against a primary database at the time of writing, it is marked “Not confirmed” or “Not ranked” rather than asserted. Book chapters and conference proceedings are indexed differently from journals; N/A means the metric does not apply to that source type.
