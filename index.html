    About

    I am a data science leader and lecturer. My research record spans AI, cyber security and collaborative systems, and my teaching covers data science, machine learning and NLP at British University Vietnam.

    I am most interested in models that do more than score well on a benchmark. They need to be explainable, robust to adversaries and verifiable by someone who does not trust the people who trained them.

    Open to collaboration on trustworthy ML, graph learning, federated learning and AI in education. The quickest way to reach me is through LinkedIn or the BUV faculty page.
